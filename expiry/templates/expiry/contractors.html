Before you get started

A few useful details.

Is this for a small HVAC, plumbing or electrical business?

Yes. This workflow is designed for a small contractor tracking multiple technician certifications and company insurance policies. Each renewal is an individual expiry item in your account.

Can I start with the list I already keep in Excel?

Yes. Add items one at a time by copying the names and dates from your sheet. This page describes manual entry; it does not offer automatic spreadsheet import.

Are my records visible on this public page?

This page uses fictional examples. Your saved items are available only through your account, and document downloads require login and ownership. Read how your data is handled.

Will I receive reminder emails?

{% if email_available %}Email delivery uses the address saved on your account. Automatic reminders also depend on the site’s reminder schedule being configured.{% else %}Email reminders are not enabled on this site yet. You can manage your dates and review upcoming renewals in the expiry list.{% endif %}

Can I check and update items from my phone?

Use your phone’s browser to log in to the same account, check dates and update items. An internet connection is required.
